# Truthstream — Company and Team Fit

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-13

## Why this project exists

A tool that takes a claim, finds evidence for and against it, and explains how strong that evidence is.

Company names below are potential skills and employer matches only; they are not known users, partners, customers or endorsers.

## OpenAI

**Relevant public areas:** Evaluation, safety and evidence-grounded agent research.

**Potential role families:** Security Engineer, Data Engineer, Research Engineer, ML Engineer, Applied Scientist or Software Engineer.

**Evidence to demonstrate:** MVP backend foundation with deterministic claim extraction, a pluggable evidence-retrieval interface, an in-memory demo retriever, evidence-weighted confidence scoring, FastAPI /health and /verify endpoints, CLI and automated tests. Demo retrieval is not proof of real-time or comprehensive fact-checking.
